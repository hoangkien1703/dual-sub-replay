DualSub guide

Learn a language with YouTube: two subtitle lines, replay any sentence, and help with every word.

1. Watch

  1. Open a YouTube video that has captions. DualSub turns captions on by itself and shows the sentences at the top of the right column.
  2. Click a sentence (in the list or over the video) to hear it again from its start.
  3. Click a word for its card: meaning, part of speech, pronunciation and, for English, its dictionary form, level and grammar. Click a second word in the same sentence to select the phrase between them.
  4. Save to vocabulary keeps the word with its sentence. Practice it from the DualSub menu → Saved words & practice.

Tip: pin DualSub so its menu is one click away: the puzzle piece 🧩 at the top right of Chrome → the pin 📌 next to DualSub.

2. Better translation with AI

Google translates each sentence on its own. AI translation reads the sentences around it too, as the Android app does, so the translation is natural and follows the speaker. It uses your own key from one of these services:

    Your key stays in this browser (unencrypted in your Chrome profile) and is sent only to the service you picked. You can change it any time in the DualSub menu.

    After saving, reload the YouTube video. The panel's status line shows AI translating…, then AI translation. Google's lines show until each AI line arrives, and sentences already translated are kept, so reopening a video costs nothing.

    3. When AI translation fails

    The panel's status line says what happened; Google's translation stays meanwhile.

    HTTP 401 / 400: invalid API keyThe key does not belong to the service you picked. Keys start with: Gemini AIza, OpenAI sk-, Claude sk-ant-, OpenRouter sk-or-.
    HTTP 503: high demandThe service is busy. DualSub asks again after 1 and 3 seconds, then every 30 seconds by itself. If it happens a lot with Gemini, type gemini-2.5-flash in the menu's Model field.
    HTTP 429: quota, credit or billingThe key's free daily limit is used up (it resets the next day), or a paid service needs credit.
    could not be reachedYour connection cannot reach the service. If you use a VPN or proxy, it has to cover the service's address too.
