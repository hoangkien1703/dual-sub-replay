Best Android apps to learn languages with YouTube. Ten apps compared.

Real YouTube videos are one of the best ways to learn a language, but the YouTube app shows one subtitle language at a time and has no dictionary. These Android apps fill the gap in different ways. We make DualSub Replay, so we link to every source and you can check our claims.

Which one should you pick?

You want to study any video, for free

DualSub Replay has no ads, no account and no limit on saved words. Pick a YouTube video, read both subtitles and tap a line to hear it again.

You want the app to plan your lessons

4You and LingoLingo pick videos for your level and add quizzes and exercises. Expect ads or a subscription.

You learn best by reading

LingQ turns a video’s transcript into a reading lesson and counts the words you know. Premium removes the free plan’s limits.

You make flashcards from everything

Migaku is built around sentence cards. DualSub Replay saves words with their sentence for free and sends them to AnkiDroid.

You learn Japanese, Chinese or English

DualSub Replay explains grammar on your phone: Japanese particles and endings, Chinese words with pinyin, and English parts of speech.

You prefer TV shows to YouTube

Lingopie streams series and films with interactive subtitles, for a monthly fee.

Try DualSub Replay in a minute

  1. Download DualSub-Replay.apk on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  2. Pick your own language and the language you are learning.
  3. Search YouTube in the app, or share a video to DualSub Replay from the YouTube app.
  4. Read both subtitles, tap a line to replay it, and tap a word to save it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Android app to learn a language with YouTube?

It depends on how you study. To study any YouTube video sentence by sentence for free, DualSub Replay is free and open source with no ads. For short guided lessons with exercises, 4You and LingoLingo turn videos into lessons. To read transcripts and track the words you know, LingQ imports YouTube videos as lessons.

Is there a free Android app to learn languages with YouTube?

Yes. DualSub Replay is free with no ads, no account and no limit on saved words. LingoTube and Video Lingo are free with ads, and 4You is free with ads and in-app purchases. LingQ, Trancy and LingoLingo have free plans or trials with paid upgrades.

Does Language Reactor have an Android app?

No. Language Reactor is a Chrome extension for desktop and laptop computers, and its team said in March 2026 that there is no mobile app. On Android, use a separate app such as DualSub Replay, Trancy or LingoTube for dual subtitles.

Is DualSub Replay the same as the Dualsub browser extension?

No. DualSub Replay is an open-source Android app by Kien Hoang, published on GitHub. Dualsub is a separate browser extension for Chrome and Firefox by Dualsub Studio. The two projects are not related.

Is DualSub Replay on Google Play?

Not yet. Download the official APK from the GitHub releases page. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time, and later updates install from the app’s menu.

Is DualSub Replay affiliated with the other apps on this page?

No. DualSub Replay is an independent open-source project. This page compares the other apps from their own store pages and public sources, which are linked below the table.
