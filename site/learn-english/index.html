Learn English with YouTube. Your language right underneath.

DualSub Replay is a free Android app that shows English captions with a translation into your language below them. Tap a sentence to hear it again, and tap a word to see what it means.

Turn any English video into a lesson

Interviews, TED talks, podcasts, vlogs and news: if a YouTube video has English captions, written by the creator or generated automatically, you can study it.

English and your language together

Every English line has its translation right under it, in Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese or any of 59 languages.

Follow every word

On eligible auto-generated captions, the word being spoken lights up, so you can follow fast speech.

Replay without scrubbing

Tap a subtitle line and the video jumps back to the start of that sentence. Listen again until you catch every word.

Tap a word to learn it

See its meaning and part of speech, and hear it pronounced. Tap a second word in the same line to look up a phrase.

Build your vocabulary

Save words with the sentence they came from, review them with spaced repetition, or export them to Anki.

Ask the AI tutor

Tap Explain the current line and the optional AI assistant explains the sentence, using your own Gemini, OpenAI, Claude or OpenRouter key.

How to learn English with YouTube on Android

  1. Download DualSub-Replay.apk and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  2. When the app starts, choose your own language and pick English as the language you are learning.
  3. Search YouTube for something you enjoy, such as “English listening practice”, a TED talk or a podcast, and open a video with captions.
  4. Read the English and the translation together. Tap a line to hear it again, and tap words you don’t know.
  5. Save useful words and review them in Practice. Progress in the menu shows your daily goal and streak.

Questions from English learners

Can I see English and my own language at the same time?

Yes. The English caption and its translation appear together under the video, or beside it in landscape. Translations go into 59 languages, including Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese and Indonesian.

Which YouTube videos work best for learning English?

Videos with captions written by the creator are the most accurate, such as TED talks and many interviews. Auto-generated English captions work too, and on eligible ones the spoken word is highlighted.

Does it work without internet?

Videos stream from YouTube, so they need a connection. Translation can run on your phone: turn off Google Translate (online) in Settings and download languages under Languages on this device.

Is it free?

Yes. DualSub Replay is free and open source, with no ads, subscriptions or accounts. The AI assistant is optional and uses your own key.
