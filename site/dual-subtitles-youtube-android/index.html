Which one should you pick?

You want to study for free, sentence by sentence

DualSub Replay is free with no limits on saved words. Tap a line to replay it and tap a word to look it up.

You want an AI tutor built in

Trancy Premium adds AI grammar analysis and speaking practice. DualSub Replay’s AI assistant is optional and uses your own key instead.

You already browse YouTube in Firefox

The Dualsub add-on puts two subtitle rows on YouTube’s mobile site without a separate app.

You only install apps from Google Play

LingoTube is free with ads and has A-B repeat. DualSub Replay is not on Google Play yet; you install it from GitHub.

You turn everything into flashcards

Migaku is built around sentence cards and spaced repetition, with a paid plan. DualSub Replay saves words for free and exports them to Anki.

You mostly study on a computer

Language Reactor works well in desktop Chrome, including on Netflix. Use a phone app alongside it when you are away from the computer.

Try dual subtitles with DualSub Replay

  1. Download DualSub-Replay.apk on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  2. Pick your own language and the language you are learning.
  3. Search YouTube in the app, or share a video to DualSub Replay from the YouTube app.
  4. Read both subtitles, tap a line to replay it, and tap a word to save it.

Frequently asked questions

Can the YouTube app show two subtitles at once?

No. The YouTube app shows one caption language at a time. To see two together on Android you need a separate app, such as DualSub Replay or Trancy, or a browser add-on such as Dualsub in Firefox.

What is the best free way to get dual subtitles on YouTube on Android?

It depends on how you study. DualSub Replay is free and open source, with tap-to-replay, word lookup and unlimited saved words. Trancy’s free plan also shows bilingual subtitles but limits saved words and keeps AI tools for Premium. Dualsub is free in Firefox’s Native Mode.

Is there an open-source app for dual subtitles on YouTube on Android?

Yes. DualSub Replay is free and open source under the MIT license, and its code is on GitHub. It shows original and translated YouTube subtitles together, replays any sentence with one tap, and has no ads and no account.

Is DualSub Replay on Google Play?

Not yet. Download the official APK from the GitHub releases page. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

Is DualSub Replay affiliated with Trancy, LingoTube, Migaku, Dualsub or Language Reactor?

No. DualSub Replay is an independent open-source project. This page compares the options from their own public pages.
