Learn from real YouTube videos, one sentence at a time

Pick any captioned video, choose your language, and study it the way you would with a textbook dialogue.

Two subtitle languages at once

Original captions and their translation sit together, so you never lose the meaning while listening.

Tap to replay

Tap a subtitle paragraph and the video jumps back to that sentence. No scrubbing.

Online or on-device translation

Google Translate online gives the best quality by default, or switch to Google ML Kit on your phone. Nothing you watch is sent to a project server.

Word-by-word highlight

On auto-generated captions, the current spoken word lights up so you can follow along.

Save words and practice

Tap a word to hear it and save it with its sentence. Review with spaced repetition or export to Anki.

Japanese grammar explained

Tap a Japanese word for its reading, meaning and part of speech, and see the grammar around it, from particles to verb endings.

Keeps playing with the screen off

Lock your phone and keep listening. Play and pause from your earphones, the lock screen or the notification.

Optional AI tutor

Ask what a line means or where a setting is. Add your own free or paid key from Gemini, OpenAI, Claude or OpenRouter; it is off until you do.

The YouTube you know

Search, Shorts, comments and recommendations stay as they are. Share any YouTube link to the app to open it.

How to watch YouTube with dual subtitles on Android

  1. Download DualSub-Replay.apk and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  2. Open DualSub Replay, pick your language, and search YouTube as usual.
  3. Choose a video with captions. The original and translated subtitles appear under the video, or beside it in landscape.
  4. Tap any subtitle paragraph to replay that sentence. Swipe the panel away to see comments and other videos.

Frequently asked questions

Learning English? See how to learn English with YouTube. Learning Japanese? See how DualSub Replay explains Japanese grammar. Comparing apps? Read the best Android apps for learning languages with YouTube or six ways to get dual subtitles on Android.

Is DualSub Replay free?

Yes. DualSub Replay is free and open source. There are no ads, subscriptions, or accounts, and no API key is needed.

How do I show two subtitles at once on YouTube for Android?

Install DualSub Replay, open a YouTube video that has captions, and choose the language you want next to the original. The app shows the original caption and its translation together under the video.

Does it work like Language Reactor on Android?

Browser extensions such as Language Reactor run in desktop Chrome, not in the Android YouTube app. DualSub Replay is a separate Android app that gives a similar two-language subtitle view for YouTube, plus tap-to-replay and saved words. It is not affiliated with Language Reactor.

Which languages are supported?

Any caption language a YouTube video offers can be the original. Translations can go into 59 languages supported by Google ML Kit, including English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, French, German, and Vietnamese.

Can I learn Japanese with DualSub Replay?

Yes. Tap a Japanese word to see its reading, meaning and part of speech, plus the grammar around it, such as the particle after it or an ending like 〜てくれました. You can hear it pronounced and save it with its sentence.

Is DualSub Replay the same as the Dualsub browser extension?

No. DualSub Replay is an open-source Android app by Kien Hoang, published on GitHub. Dualsub is a separate browser extension for Chrome and Firefox by Dualsub Studio. The two projects are not related.

Is DualSub Replay on Google Play?

Not yet. Download the official APK from the GitHub releases page. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

Does translation send my subtitles to a server?

By default, subtitle text goes to Google Translate for the best quality. Turn off Google Translate (online) in Settings → Translation and Google ML Kit translates on your phone instead. The project runs no server and collects no analytics.
