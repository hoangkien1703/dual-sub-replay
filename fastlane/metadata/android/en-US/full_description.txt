<p>DualSub Replay helps you learn languages with YouTube. It shows the original captions and a translation together, and lets you tap any sentence to hear that exact moment again.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Browse and search the mobile YouTube site inside the app, or share a YouTube link to it.</li>
<li>Load manual or auto-generated public captions automatically.</li>
<li>Show original and translated subtitles side by side in a scrollable timeline.</li>
<li>Tap a subtitle paragraph to jump back and replay it.</li>
<li>Follow the spoken word on eligible auto-generated captions.</li>
<li>Landscape split view with a resizable subtitle panel.</li>
<li>Tap a word to hear it, save it with its sentence, and review it later with spaced repetition.</li>
<li>Export saved words as JSON or Anki-ready TSV.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>No app account, analytics, ads, or project server. Saved words and settings stay on your device, and translation runs on the device.</p>
<p>Besides YouTube, the app connects only to download files it needs, once: the translation model for each language from Mozilla, and, the first time Japanese subtitles load, a Japanese dictionary from Maven Central (or Google's mirror of it). No subtitle text is sent. Signing in to YouTube is optional and happens on Google's own sign-in page inside the app.</p>

<p><b>Good to know</b></p>
<p>The app uses YouTube's website and caption data, which are not free services. Captions come from an undocumented YouTube interface that can change without notice.</p>
